Drive Practice

Support

Drive Practice is a calm game for learning how a car moves in tight spaces: turning, reversing, parking and getting round pillars.

Get help or report a problem

Found a bug, stuck on a level or have an idea? Open an issue on GitHub and describe what happened. Mention your device and the level if you can.

Open an issue

Common questions

How do I steer? Drag the big wheel round, pick D or R, then hold the gas pedal with your other hand. On a computer, use the mouse for the wheel and W/S or the arrow keys for the pedals.

Where is my progress saved? On this device only. Deleting the app removes it.

Can I put the wheel on the other side? Yes, use the switch at the bottom of the level list.

Can I change the language? Yes, at the top of the level list. The game speaks English, Spanish, German, Polish and Chinese.
